How it works

  • DOI present in the reference: the DOI is fetched directly and the metadata is compared with the text of your reference. A DOI that resolves to a different paper is flagged.
  • No DOI: the reference text is sent to Crossref's bibliographic search and the top three results are scored.
  • Scoring: title similarity, first-author surname, and publication year are each checked. Match means all three agree, Likely means most agree, Unverified means Crossref returned nothing close, and Mismatch means a DOI pointed to a different work.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is stored on a server. Requests go only to api.crossref.org.
  • Crossref covers most journal articles and many books, but not everything. An Unverified result means the reference could not be found, not that it is wrong. Check it by hand.
